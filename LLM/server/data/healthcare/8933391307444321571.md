CMS price transparency
The Centers for Medicare and Medicaid Services (CMS) requires hospitals to make available online a listing of standard charges using a CMS adopted layout with charge information encoded in accordance with CMS technical specifications.
Mayo Clinic supports the intent of these regulations to educate patients on the cost of healthcare and patient financial responsibilities. Mayo Clinic has made a good faith effort to ensure the data in the price transparency files is true, accurate and complete.
To request a formal estimation of services, please contact us at
833-479-5483
(domestic patients) or
1-507-284-5063
(international patients). Office hours are 8 a.m. to 5 p.m. Central time, Monday through Friday.
Please select the appropriate facility for the listing of charges you are seeking:
Mayo Clinic — Phoenix, Arizona
CMS Price Transparency Machine Readable File
Mayo Clinic — Jacksonville, Florida
CMS Price Transparency Machine Readable File
Mayo Clinic — Rochester, Minnesota
CMS Price Transparency Machine Readable File
