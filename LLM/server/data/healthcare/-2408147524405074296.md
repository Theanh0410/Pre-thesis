International Patient Services at Mayo Clinic
International locations
International locations
Representative offices
Mayo Clinic has several offices for international residents who wish to visit Mayo Clinic. Staff who speak your language will answer your questions and help you request appointments and make travel arrangements.
These offices do not provide medical care.
Mayo Clinic representative offices are located in:
Canada
China
Colombia
Dominican Republic
Ecuador
El Salvador
Guatemala
Honduras
India
Indonesia
Mexico
Panama
Peru
United Arab Emirates
Uruguay
Independent referral facilitators in China
Mayo Clinic works with independent referral organizations in China that facilitate the referral process for patients to all its campuses, including making travel arrangements. These recognized referral facilitators have worked with Mayo Clinic to establish a reliable process for assisting patients who wish to visit Mayo Clinic for healthcare services.

independent referral facilitator
Mayo Clinic Healthcare in London
Mayo Clinic Healthcare
provides a variety of services in central London, including an Executive Health Program, comprehensive health assessments, specialty consultations, second opinions and diagnostic services outside the U.K.'s National Health Service. It also is a gateway to more than 7,300 Mayo Clinic experts in the U.S.
