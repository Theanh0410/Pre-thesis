TEMPUR-Adapt Mattress
Shop Now
Tempur-Pedic burst onto the scene in the 1990s as the first company to integrate memory foam into mattresses. Since then, the company has continued growing and innovating to offer a wide selection of mattress models featuring TEMPUR memory foam.
The TEMPUR-Adapt is an 11-inch mattress that comes in two models. One is made entirely of foam, while the other has a support core of pocketed innerspring coils. Both have a firmness level of medium (5).
As with other Tempur-Pedic mattresses, the Adapt models are pricier than many other brands. We wanted to help determine if the performance justifies the price, so we brought the TEMPUR-Adapt into our in-house Test Lab.
We go over all of our findings, detailing the strengths and weaknesses of the TEMPUR-Adapt Mattress. We also cover its design and important policies related to shipping and returns, all of which may influence your decision about whether to buy the TEMPUR-Adapt.

Side Sleepers
The TEMPUR-Adapt Mattress is a natural fit for most side sleepers under 230 pounds, and in our testing, both the hybrid and all-foam models got particularly rave reviews from those who weigh less than 130 pounds.
People in this sleeping position need plenty of cushioning for their hips and shoulders. The TEMPUR foam provides the kind of tailored contouring that can accommodate those pressure points. This minimizes impact against the mattress surface and helps keep the spine aligned.
However, side sleepers over 230 pounds tend to push too far into the foam, which can actually make it harder to maintain a neutral spinal position.
Back Sleepers
Among back sleepers in our Test Lab, those under 130 pounds were most satisfied by the feel and support of the TEMPUR-Adapt. While some back sleepers who weigh between 130 and 230 pounds got sufficient support, they tended to complain that there was a lack of firmness.
Back sleepers during our testing preferred the hybrid model because the coils give it a bit more pushback. Nevertheless, most back sleepers over 130 pounds said that this wouldn’t be a top mattress choice, especially given its price-point.
Stomach Sleepers
Throughout our testing, stomach sleepers experienced a lack of support on the TEMPUR-Adapt Mattress.
Too much conforming can make stomach sleepers feel like they are being swallowed by the mattress, and, even worse, can cause the midsection to sag out of alignment with the rest of the body. This affected stomach sleepers over 230 pounds the most, but even those with a lighter body weight said they would prefer a firmer mattress.

“I was impressed by how well the TEMPUR-Adapt Mattress absorbed vibrations, which makes it much easier to keep sleeping soundly.” – Danny
The TEMPUR-Adapt is highly effective at isolating motion thanks to its proprietary
memory foam
, which absorbs vibrations and prevents them from spreading across the bed.
In our tests, the foam models provided the highest level of motion isolation, while the hybrid model, with its coil support system, transferred some light movement.
Any model would work well for couples, but for those seeking the best motion isolation, the all-foam option is ideal.
Pressure Relief
8.5
/ 10
“Some sleepers might find the contouring on this mattress to be overkill, but I found that it was a source of significant relief for pressure points like my shoulders and hips.” – Jeremy
TEMPUR memory foam is known for its ability to ease pressure by conforming to the body’s shape, reducing pain at contact points and providing extra support exactly where it’s needed.
However, individual comfort and support needs can vary based on body type and sleeping style. Some may find the contouring of this mattress to be too enveloping, causing excessive sinkage that can disrupt spinal alignment. This is especially true for stomach sleepers and those weighing over 230 pounds.
For that reason, while we give the Adapt high scores for pressure relief, it’s important to make sure its characteristics suit your needs.

TEMPUR-Adapt Mattress Review Breakdown
The TEMPUR-Adapt Mattress has three interior layers that combine to make the mattress 11 inches tall. There are two models available, and both have a firmness level of medium (5).
The two models share a number of design elements. Both models have a stretchy cover intended to feel smooth and soft, and two top layers made with TEMPUR memory foam.
TEMPUR foam is well known for its contouring properties that allow it to relieve pressure and cushion the joints. This material also has a reputation for excellent motion isolation.
The all-foam model has a support core made of high-density polyfoam. By contrast, the
hybrid model’s
support core is composed of thick metal coils. The pocketed coil system gives each spring an independent range of motion in order to offer more tailored support and cut down on disruptions when a sleeping partner moves.

Trial, Warranty, and Shipping Policies
Availability
The TEMPUR-Adapt can be purchased by shoppers in any of the 50 states. It is sold directly on Tempur-Pedic’s website, and it is also available from many third-party mattress retailers both online and in-store.
Shipping
The TEMPUR-Adapt comes with free White Glove delivery within the contiguous United States. Delivery is usually scheduled within two weeks after you place an order.
For White Glove delivery, a local company will schedule a date and time. They install the TEMPUR-Adapt and can remove an old mattress as well.
This service is available in Hawaii and Alaska for a $600 charge. It typically takes four to six weeks for the mattress to be delivered to these states.
Sleep Trial
There is a 90-night sleep trial for the TEMPUR-Adapt. The company requires that you try the mattress for a minimum of 30 nights.
Returns are subject to a return shipping fee. If you request a return, Tempur-Pedic will pick up the mattress from your home.
Warranty
The limited warranty for the TEMPUR-Adapt lasts for 10 years. Tempur-Pedic will repair or replace the mattress if there are any major defects in the materials, provided that the damage is not related to improper use or care. You may be required to pay for shipping related to warranty service.
