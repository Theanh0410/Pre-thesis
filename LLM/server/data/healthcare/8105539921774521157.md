Product Research Methodology: Our Process
Our team at Sleep Foundation conducts extensive hands-on tests for each mattress to provide you with the most reliable, data-driven recommendations and reviews. Below are key guidelines that inform our testing methodology:
Our testers lie on the surface of each mattress to evaluate factors such as contouring and support. We also use specialized tools to measure performance criteria like pressure relief, heat retention, and motion isolation.
We test each mattress using various sleep positions to observe whether a mattress is better suited for side, back, or stomach sleeping. We roll around the surface and sit on the edges to gauge ease of movement and perimeter support.
We have tested hundreds of mattresses and bring years of experience in the mattress industry to the test lab. Our testing team covers a wide range of sleep preferences, sleep positions, and body types. This helps us represent as many sleeper types as possible in our reviews.

The WinkBed comes in four firmness options, so there’s a model to suit all sleep positions. Side sleepers gravitate toward the plusher models for pressure relief while back and stomach sleepers usually prefer the support of the firmer models. Factor in body weight when determining your ideal firmness option. Overly firm mattresses contribute to pressure buildup while mattresses that are too soft can lead to poor spinal alignment.
Side Sleepers
The Softer version of the WinkBed is best suited for side sleepers who weigh up to 130 pounds. The Luxury Firm is most comfortable for those who weigh between 130 and 230 pounds and the Firmer and Plus options should be a good fit for most side sleepers who weigh over 230 pounds.
The Softer, Luxury Firm, and Firmer models have a Euro-top design that cushions the surface, which is ideal for pressure relief in the shoulders and hips. Meanwhile a zoned coil core gives extra support near the midsection to promote proper spinal alignment. The Plus features a polyfoam layer for cushioning and zoned latex and coil layers for added support and pressure relief.
Back Sleepers
Back sleepers need a bed that cushions the spine without sacrificing support. If you weigh under 130 pounds, you’ll find this balance in the Softer and Luxury Firm models. Back sleepers between 130 and 230 pounds should feel most comfortable with the Luxury Firm and Firmer models, while those over 230 pounds receive the most benefits from the extra support of the Firmer and Plus options.
Every version of the WinkBed Mattress features zoning in the coils to prevent uncomfortable sinkage in the hips. Since the Plus version is constructed for larger individuals, it also uses zoning in its latex layer for extra support where back sleepers need it most.
Stomach Sleepers
Since people carry much of their body weight near their bellies and hips, stomach sleepers usually need extra support to prevent the midsection from sagging into the mattress. All of the WinkBed models offer enhanced support in the hips, which is ideal for stomach sleepers.
Stomach sleepers under 130 pounds receive just the right amount of support and hip cushioning from the Luxury Firm model, while those between 130 and 230 pounds felt comfortable on the three firmer models. Stomach sleepers over 230 pounds fared best on the Firmer and Plus options. The Softer WinkBed is too plush for most stomach sleepers.

My husband is a big snorer and I am six months pregnant, so we both move quite a bit throughout the night these days. While it’s not perfect, I think the WinkBed does a good job isolating movement enough so we’re not constantly waking each other up. — Logan
All four firmness options of the WinkBed have better motion isolation than many other hybrid models we’ve tested. To measure motion isolation, our testers observe the amount of movement felt when another tester moves across the surface — and when a kettle ball is dropped onto the bed.
The Softer, Luxury Firm, and Firmer options have multiple layers of polyfoam that significantly absorb vibrations, preventing motion from traveling across the surface. The Softer model absorbs even more motion because of its soft feel and extra polyfoam layer. Although the coil core gives the mattress some bounce, the foam layers are substantial enough to dampen motion transfer.

Like with many hybrid models, the WinkBed’s strong contouring can help relieve painful pressure points. The Plus version uses polyfoam and latex layers, which offer gentle contouring as well.

The Softer, Luxury Firm, and Firmer options feature polyfoam infused with cooling gel, which is engineered to minimize heat retention. WinkBed Mattress Review Breakdown
The WinkBed is a hybrid model with four firmness options, including medium soft (4), medium firm (6), firm (7), and firm (8). The construction and materials vary between these firmness options.
Each WinkBed Mattress uses a Tencel cover. This natural fabric is derived from eucalyptus and is crafted to sleep cool by wicking away excessive heat.
The Softer, Luxury Firm, and Firmer versions of the mattress have a quilted Euro-top with gel-infused polyfoam followed by another polyfoam layer. This material contours to the sleeper’s body to relieve pressure points, and the gel infusion pulls heat away. The Softer version has an extra polyfoam layer in the Euro-top to enhance cushioning.
The Plus option has a unique comfort system with a layer of dense polyfoam and a 2.5-inch layer of latex. The density of the polyfoam makes it more durable and less likely to form lasting impressions. The latex layer provides a responsive feel and enables excellent airflow.
Each model has a pocketed coil system with a zoned design to provide targeted support and proper spinal alignment. The pocketing of the coils reduces motion transfer. The Plus option bands these pocketed coils together in groups of four for extra support.
All of the firmness options measure 13.5 inches thick, making the WinkBed a high-profile model. Because it is thicker than average, the bed may require extra deep fitted sheets.

Trial, Warranty, and Shipping Policies
Availability
The WinkBed Mattress ships within the United States. It is available through the WinkBeds website.
You can also try the WinkBed Mattress at six brick-and-mortar locations, including the SleePare Showrooms in New York City, Tysons Corner, VA, and Miami, FL; the Sleep Sherpa Showrooms in Edina, MN; the Sandman Sleep store in Chicago, IL; and the Our Sleep Guide Showrooms in Austin and Houston, TX.
Shipping
WinkBeds provides free ground shipping in the contiguous U.S. The mattress ships via UPS and arrives compressed as a bed in a box designed for maneuverability. Purchasers are responsible for moving the mattress to their room of choice, unpackaging it, and setting it up.
Mattresses are assembled once an order is placed. Shipping usually takes about two weeks, though the precise arrival date may vary based partially on where you are located.
Sleep Trial
A 120-night sleep trial allows buyers to try the mattress in their homes. The purchaser must try the bed for at least 30 nights before they can return it for a full refund.
Buyers can also exchange their mattress if they discover it isn’t their ideal firmness. Customers who exchange their mattresses will receive a 60-night trial period for their replacement mattress.
This offer is limited to one trial period per customer and shipping address.
Warranty
The WinkBed Mattress comes with a lifetime limited warranty. This warranty is valid for the original mattress owner who purchased their WinkBed from an authorized retailer. The owner should retain a copy of the receipt as proof of purchase.
This warranty covers defects, including indentations greater than 1.5 inches, defects in the assembly of the cover, and flaws that cause degradation, splitting, or cracking of the foam. The warranty does not cover damage that results from abuse or neglect, and the mattress must be used on a proper foundation. Eligible defects will be remedied with a mattress replacement at no cost to the customer.
Customers are responsible for the shipping costs associated with returning the mattress to WinkBeds. If WinkBeds confirms a qualifying defect, it will cover the transportation costs.
