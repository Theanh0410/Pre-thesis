Financial services
Mayo Clinic provides services to help you with financial considerations and explore available payment options to receive our care. We have direct payment contracts with a variety of insurers that provide international health coverage. Our dedicated staff of international account representatives can assist you and your family with financial details before, during and after your visit to Mayo Clinic.
Services include:
Information about deposits and payment.
Fee estimates and fee updates for medical services ordered by a Mayo Clinic physician.
Mayo Clinic's
cost estimator tool
.
Mayo Clinic strives to provide timely, accurate and concise billing services to patients and their families. Our staff can assist you with any financial and insurance questions.
