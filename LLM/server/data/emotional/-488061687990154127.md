About
Steve Mazza, Ph.D.,
is an Assistant Professor of Medical Psychology (in Psychiatry) at Columbia University Medical Center within the Division of Child and Adolescent Psychiatry. As a Licensed Clinical Psychologist and Clinical Director of the ABC Early Childhood Program at ColumbiaDoctors, he specializes in treating early childhood disorders and anxiety disorders across the lifespan.
Mazza earned a B.S. in elementary education from Skidmore College, an M.A. in developmental psychology from Columbia University, and a Ph.D. in clinical psychology from Hofstra University. He completed his post-doctoral fellowship at the Columbia University Clinic for Anxiety and Related Disorders (CUCARD), where he specialized in providing Cognitive Behavioral Therapy (CBT) and Acceptance and Commitment Therapy (ACT) to children, adolescents, and young adults. He is a certified Parent-Child Interaction Therapy (PCIT) trainer and therapist.
Mazza provides expert guidance to parents, educators, summer camps, and leadership teams as a consultant and public speaker. He has been featured on SiriusXM Doctor Radio, HealthLine, InsideHook, PsychHub, and Camping Magazine.
Beyond his clinical work, Mazza is the co-founder of two nonprofit organizations: the Early Childhood Provider Network (ECPN), a collaborative initiative that empowers early childhood professionals to provide optimal care and support for young children, and Play It Forward, a nonprofit organization that harnesses the power of music to drive social impact.
