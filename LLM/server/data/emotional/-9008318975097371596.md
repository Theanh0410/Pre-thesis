Psychology Today does not capture or store personally identifiable information, and your identity cannot be determined from your responses. Aggregated self-test responses are stored to improve the tests and provide performance comparisons. If you wish to remove cookies from this site or opt out of data sharing, please click 'Do not share or sell my personal information' below. For a reliable medical diagnosis, please consult a professional.
Who Is This ADHD Test For?
This test is for anyone who notices signs of distractibility, inattention, restlessness, or impulsivity that are interfering with their ability to function in day-to-day life; it is not intended to be diagnostic. It is designed for adults.

Get Help With ADHD
If you think you may be experiencing symptoms of ADHD, speak to a healthcare professional. Make an appointment either with your primary care physician or a licensed mental health provider; you can find one in your area on the
