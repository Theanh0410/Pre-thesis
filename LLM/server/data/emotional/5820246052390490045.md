About
T. Alexander Puutio, Ph.D.,
teaches organizational performance and leadership at Harvard and Columbia and is a seasoned leadership expert, founder, and management consultant specializing in organizational performance. His work explores how curiosity, range, and purposeful leadership drive individual and collective flourishing. With a background in behavioral science, strategic consulting, and executive advising, he has helped global leaders and organizations navigate complexity, build adaptive cultures, and optimize for long-term success. Puutio’s insights have been featured in Forbes, Fast Company, and WEF, among other outlets, and he collaborates with leading thinkers to bridge research and real-world application in leadership and organizational psychology.
