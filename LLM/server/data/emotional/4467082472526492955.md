About
Amy Campbell, MClinPsych,
is a clinical psychologist and sex educator who helps individuals and couples navigate intimacy, desire, and connection without the awkwardness, shame, or guesswork. With over a decade of experience, she combines evidence-based therapy with mindfulness strategies to tackle common struggles like mismatched libido, performance anxiety, and feeling disconnected in relationships.
Campbell also works with therapists and professionals, offering training and talks that make it easier to bring sexual wellness into the therapy room with confidence (because let’s be honest, this stuff wasn’t covered in grad school). She is the author of
The Mindful Sex Guide
, a no-BS, science-backed approach to unlocking pleasure and deepening intimacy. Her work is about making these conversations easier, more accessible, and grounded in real, practical strategies so people can move past frustration and into connection.
