How agreeable are you?
Agreeableness—the tendency to be cooperative, accommodating, trusting, and friendly—is a prosocial personality trait that is a strong predictor of happiness and well-being, but that can be problematic when taken to extremes. Take this quiz to find out how agreeable you are.

Who Is This Agreeableness Test For?
This test is for anyone who desires to know how agreeable they are and how the trait might be influencing their life. This test is designed for adults and is not intended to be diagnostic.

How to Become More Agreeable
If low agreeableness—or excessively high agreeableness—is interfering with your relationships and your emotional well-being, seeking the help of a therapist can be a powerful tool for change. Find a professional near you on the
