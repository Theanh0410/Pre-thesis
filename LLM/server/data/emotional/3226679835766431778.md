About
Moshe Ratson, MBA, LMFT,
is a psychotherapist and executive coach in New York City. As a leading voice in the world of personal and professional development, he specializes in anger management, emotional intelligence, infidelity issues, performance management, and couples and marriage therapy. His unique approach to anger and emotional intelligence has been applied effectively to the fields of business leadership and organizational development. In addition, he coaches individuals and teams at Fortune 100 companies, government agencies, and leading nonprofits.
Ratson holds an MBA degree as well as an MS degree in Marriage and Family Therapy (MFT). He is a Licensed Marriage and Family Therapist (LMFT), a clinical member of the American Association of Marriage and Family Therapy (AAMFT), and a member of the International Coach Federation (ICF). Before becoming a psychotherapist, Ratson was a professional basketball player, a basketball coach, and a military officer. He has many years of experience as a business executive and a solid track record of managing organizations, sales, marketing, and business development teams.
