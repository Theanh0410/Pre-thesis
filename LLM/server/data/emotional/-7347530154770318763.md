Experience
Claudia de Llano is a Marriage and Family Therapist, lecturer, mental health speaker, and author of "The Seven Destinies of Love." She facilitates peoples’ paths toward awakening of inner harmony and conscious being.
An uplifting storyteller and educator, she has delivered numerous presentations and workshops in private and professional settings around the globe. By blending psychology and business acumen, she provides inspiring and transformational experiences to help audiences and participants integrate and expand their emotional, personal, and professional lives.
As a clinician, her gentle and thought-provoking approach aims to awaken the connections we seek to live fulfilled and balanced lives. She engages by honoring who people already are, honoring their beliefs, values, and strengths in hopes of helping individuals channel their innate gifts into living inspiration.
Education
Claudia holds a Master’s Degree in Psychology with a specialization in multicultural training. Her expertise lies in systemic and strength-based approaches. Currently, she is a lecturer at California State University Northridge and Phillips Graduate Institute. She is also a certified yoga and meditation and sound healing practitioner integrating complementary therapies and techniques into her work. Her recent corporate engagements include topics of Resilience, Well-Being, and Stress Reduction.
