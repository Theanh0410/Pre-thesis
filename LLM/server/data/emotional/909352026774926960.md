Ed Thompson
About
Ed Thompson
is the founder and CEO of Uptimize, a unique corporate training platform that helps organizations attract, hire, and retain talent that thinks differently. Educated at the University of Oxford, Thompson founded Uptimize in 2016, after recognizing an urgent need for greater understanding and appreciation of neurodiversity within the working world.
He is a frequent speaker on the topic of neurodiversity, and has been featured by outlets including LinkedIn, the BBC, People Management magazine, HR.com, and the Financial Times. Thompson is the author of
A Hidden Force: Unlocking the Potential of Neurodiversity at Work.
