Are you a hopeless romantic?
The hopeless romantic weeps when watching a tearjerker, and believes in love at first sight, knowing that love will conquer all. Sounds great, but this could mean they may not spot red flags in a new relationship. At the other end of the scale, the highly unromantic person is businesslike and they do not believe in happily-ever-after fairy tales.

Who Is This Romantic Personality Test For?
This test is for anyone who feels they need to rein in their tendency to love blindly, and it is also for people who feel they are too businesslike and lack intimacy in their relationships. This test is designed for adults and is not intended to be diagnostic.
