Ankita Guchait MBPsS
About
Ankita Guchait, MBPsS,
is a London-based Mental Health Practitioner and Clinical Data Analyst, specializing in supporting refugees and neurodiverse children. She conducts trauma screenings and provides mental health care to assist refugees in adapting to new environments while offering tailored assessments and interventions for children with neurodiverse conditions. In addition to her clinical work, she actively contributes to mental health research. Her career is driven by a strong commitment to humanitarian efforts, shaped by personal experiences and a global perspective gained from working across the U.S., UK, Russia, and India.
