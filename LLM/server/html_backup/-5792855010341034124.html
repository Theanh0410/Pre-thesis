Azadeh Aalai, Ph.D., is an assistant professor of Psychology at Queensborough Community College in New York.

Mitch Abblett, Ph.D., is a keynote speaker, author, and clinician who helps others own more connection and impact in the raw matter of life ... moments.

Allison Abrams, LCSW-R, is a licensed psychotherapist in NYC, as well as a writer and advocate for mental health awareness and destigmatization.

Mitch Abrams, Psy.D. is a clinical sport psychologist specializing in anger management, sexual assault prevention and the treatment of trauma.

Shawn Abreu, MD, practices hospice and palliative medicine and serves as a medical director at Hospice of the Valley in Phoenix. He is the co-author of Death and Dying.

Josh Ackerman, Ph.D., is a professor of social psychology working at the University of Michigan.

Christine B. L. Adams, MD is a child psychiatrist in private practice in Louisville, KY. She is co-author of the book Living on Automatic: How Emotional Conditioning Shapes Our Lives and Relationships.

Kathryn Betts Adams, Ph.D., M.S.W., is a former associate professor of social work at Case Western Reserve University. Now living in the Hartford, Connecticut, area, she writes and consults about mid-life and aging.

Patti Adler, Ph.D., is Professor of Sociology at the University of Colorado, Boulder. Peter Adler, Ph.D., is Professor of Sociology at the University of Denver.

Marina Adshade, Ph.D., teaches at the Vancouver School of Economics at the University of British Columbia.

Amir A. Afkhami, M.D., Ph.D. DFAPA, is a professor of psychiatry, global health, and history at George Washington University and currently serves as the Vice Chair for Clinical Affairs and the Director of Medical Student Education at the GW Department of Psychiatry.

J. Ibeh Agbanyim, Ph.D., is an industrial-organizational psychology practitioner and the author of The Five Principles of Collaboration.

Leah Aguirre, LCSW, is a psychotherapist based in San Diego, CA. She works primarily with individuals who have experienced complex trauma and struggle with mental health challenges.

Nina Ahuja, M.D., is a surgeon and founder of Docs in Leadership, healing and transforming culture through neuroleadership, and author of memoir Stress in Medicine.

Chamin Ajjan, MS, LCSW, ACT is a licensed psychotherapist in NYC, specializing in intimacy and relationships, and the author of Seeking Soulmate: Ditch The Dating Game and Find Real Connection.

Nafees Alam, Ph.D., is a professor specializing in nonprofit program evaluation and macro practice, where he has over seven years of experience.

Max Alberhasky, Ph.D., is an Assistant Professor of Marketing at California State University Long Beach. His research examines consumer psychology and the interplay between money, social relationships, and happiness.

Michael Alcee, Ph.D., is a clinical psychologist, TEDx speaker, and author who specializes in using his background in music, literature, and the arts to showcase the transformative power of psychotherapy.

Kelly-Ann Allen, Ph.D., FAPS, is an Associate Professor and educational and developmental psychologist at Monash University, a fellow of the College of Educational and Developmental Psychologists, and an honorary principal fellow of the University of Melbourne.

Mary Allen, the author of The Rooms of Heaven, is the recipient of a National Endowment for the Arts fellowship.

Michael Allison is a polyvagal performance consultant and educational partner with Polyvagal Institute. His methodology, which applies polyvagal theory to optimize health, resilience, and performance, is endorsed by Stephen W. Porges, Ph.D.

Pam Allyn is the Executive Director and founder of LitWorld, a non-profit literacy organization, and LitLife, a national organization dedicated to school improvement.

Carlos Alós-Ferrer, Ph.D., is the Chair Professor in Economics at Lancaster University Management School and the editor-in-chief of the Journal of Economic Psychology.

Aigerim Alpysbekova, MPH, is a researcher and advocate at the University of Texas at Austin, specializing in the psychological impacts of trauma on displaced populations.
