Ali Shehab

About

Ali Shehab is an award-winning journalist and creative content strategist with a passion for making psychology accessible to diverse audiences. He holds a Master’s degree in Organizational Psychology from the Lebanese University. Ali has conducted extensive research on remote work dynamics and its impact on worker-employer relationships in Lebanon.

Ali’s professional journey spans over a decade, combining expertise in psychology, media, and technology. He has authored articles for prestigious platforms such as Harvard Business Review Arabia and MIT Technology Review Arabia and runs Mellivora, a psychology-focused newsletter exploring geopolitics, media, and technology from a psychological perspective.

A fluent communicator in Arabic, English, and French, Ali bridges the gap between psychological theory and practical application, empowering individuals and organizations to thrive.
